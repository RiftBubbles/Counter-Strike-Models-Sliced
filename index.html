Pre-Sliced Counter-Strike Weapon Models!

My first CS2 skin took 5 days—2 of which were wasted fighting Valve's welded meshes and hunting down overlapping triangles. I originally built this library just for myself, but I decided to share it so creators can skip the mesh prep and start painting in seconds with 1:1 UVs and proper materials.

Why use these models?

Everything is broken down into organized, named groups—stock, barrel, frame, receiver—so you can finish your custom skin in minutes.

These models use the original 1:1 UV maps and scale — the only difference is that I’ve taken them apart for you.

The AK-47 is broken down into nine main sections:

  • A — Top Handguard
  • B — Bottom Handguard
  • C — Top Cover
  • D — Barrel
  • E — Upper Barrel
  • F — Lower Dust Cover
  • G — Upper Dust Cover
  • H — Magazine
  • I — Internal Parts

Part counts vary from weapon to weapon. The AK47 has 15 total parts in the full collection.

Quick-Start Guide

  1. Download the weapon archive (.zip)
  2. Extract the files
  3. Import or drag into Blender
  4. Start painting

It's best to drag the files into a blender project.

.BLEND files already have pre-assigned materials for instant drag-and-paint.

FBX files include raw geometry without preset materials for use outside of Blender.
